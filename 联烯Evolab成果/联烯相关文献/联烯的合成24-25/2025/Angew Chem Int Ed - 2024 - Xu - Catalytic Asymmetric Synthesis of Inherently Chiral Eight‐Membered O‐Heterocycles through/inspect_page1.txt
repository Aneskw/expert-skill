      "text": "Organocatalysis V ery Important Paper\nCatalytic Asymmetric Synthesis of Inherently Chiral Eight-Membered\nO-Heterocycles through Cross-[4 + 4] Cycloaddition of Quinone\nMethides\nDa Xu, Guojie Zhou, Bangli Liu, Shiqi Jia, Yidong Liu,* and Hailong Yan*\nAbstract: Inherently chiral eight-membered rings embedded in tetraphenylene derivatives and hetero-analogues exhibit\nunique properties and allow diverse applications. A conceptually viable and straightforward approach to these\nframeworks is [4+4] cycloaddition, which still remains elusive. Herein, we describe the stereoselective cross-[4+4]\ncycloaddition of quinone methides (QMs), leading to the formation of oxa-analogues of tetraphenylene with exceptional\nchemo-, diastereo-, and enantioselectivity. The structures of these novel rigid eight-membered O-heterocycles were\nexplored by single-crystal X-ray diffraction, and their stereochemical stability was elaborated through both density\nfunctional theory (DFT) calculations and thermal racemization experiments. The developed methodology exhibited\nbroad substrate scope and the resulting cross-[4+4] cycloadducts could be readily transformed into valuable chiral\nbuilding blocks. Our findings expand the library of inherently chiral medium-sized rings and also contribute to the\nadvancement of asymmetric cross-[4+4] cycloadditions of quinone methides.\nIntroduction\nChirality is an intrinsic property of molecular systems. The\ndesign of new chiral molecular architectures and stereo-\nchemical control are significant for asymmetric synthesis.\n[1]\nTetraphenylene with a rigid central eight-membered ring is\nformed by ortho-annulation of four benzenes.\n[2–5]\nThe entire\nmolecule with suitable substituents can create inherent\nchirality,\n[6–16]\nwhich is distinct from the prevailing stereogenic\nelements such as central, axial, planar, and helical elements.\nDue to their unique chiral properties, tetraphenylene\nderivatives are used as functional molecules and have\nattracted considerable attention.\n[17–19]\nHowever, only Shibata,\nYang, and Zhu reported sporadic asymmetric synthetic\napproaches for the construction of chiral tetraphenylene\nderivatives and their aza-analogues (Figure 1A).\n[20–24]\nNota-\nbly, the challenge escalates when oxa-analogues are used as\ntarget molecules. The incorporation of oxygen into the\ncentral eight-membered ring increases the conformational\nflexibility, so the inherently chiral eight-membered O-\nheterocycles are elusive. Hence, it is significant to enhance\nits inversion barrier by the structural modification of oxa-\nanalogue and develop the corresponding asymmetric cata-\nlytic method of enantioenriched oxa-analogues of tetraphe-\nnylene.\nThe [4+4] cycloaddition is conceptually recognized as a\nstraightforward and atom-economical approach towards the\neight-membered rings,\n[25,26]\nand has been successfully used to\nforge chiral cyclooctadiene products by Dieck, Cramer, and\nJørgensen.\n[27–29]\nNevertheless, the asymmetric synthesis of\neight-membered O-heterocycles through thermal [4+4]\ncycloaddition was seldom reported due to the lack of\nsynthetic strategies and suitable cycloaddends. ortho-\nQuinone methides (o-QMs) are highly reactive oxygenated\nfour-atom synthons in synthetic chemistry, medicinal\nchemistry, and material chemistry.\n[30–34]\nThe strong tendency\nof rearomatization makes them strong electrophiles and\nelectron-poor heterodienes and valuable catalytic asymmet-\nric reactions involving o-QMs or its analogues have been\nrealized in recent years (Figure 1B).\n[35–53]\nThrough the judi-\ncious choice of reagents, the asymmetric [4 + 3], [4 + 2], and\n[4+1] cycloadditions of o-QMs were well established.\n[54–67]\nThe [4+4] cycloadditions of o-QMs are still underdeveloped,\nespecially the cross-[4+4] cycloaddition of o-QMs (Fig-\nure 1B).\n[68,69]\nExcept the control of the rapid reactivity of o-\nQMs, the above challenge may also arise from the unfavor-\nable entropic and transannular interactions in the generation\nof medium-sized rings.\n[70–87]\nOur group has been devoted to the development of\nasymmetric reactions by manipulating vinylidene ortho-\nquinone methides (VQMs), an o-QM analogue with an\nallene motif. With suitable substituent patterns, VQMs\ncould retain the chiral feature and act as axially chiral\nintermediates in asymmetric reactions.\n[88–107]\nWe aim to\nsynthesize inherently chiral oxa-analogues of tetraphenylene\nthrough a conceptually straightforward and atom-econom-\nical cross-[4+4] cycloaddition of VQM and typical o-QM.\n[*] D.
