Allene\nsubstrates with electron-donating or electron-withdrawing\ngroups introduced at various positions on the aromatic ring\ncan provide the corresponding products (3a−3h) with an er\nvalue as high as 97:3. To showcase the practical application of our method,\nthe allenic C(sp\n2\n)−H cyanation reaction was scaled up to a 10\nmmol batch. The process\ndemonstrates the well tolerance for diverse functional groups,\nincluding phenyl (5a−5c), naphthyl (5d), benzothiophene\n(5e), and secondary and tertiary alkyl groups (5f and 5g).\nProducts 5h−5j can also be obtained in good yields as well as\nwith higher enantioselectivities by our method.\n12a,13a\nMore-\nover, scaling up the reaction has led to a further enhancement\nin enantioselectivity, as evidenced by product 5c, which\nachieved an enantiomeric ratio of 96:4.\nFurther Transformations.
