ABSTRACT: The asymmetric total synthesis of janthinoid A has been accomplished for the first time in 14 steps without using a protecting group. The trans-decalin subunit and the rigid oxabicyclo[3.2.1]octane motif were constructed via an epoxide-initiated cationic π-cyclization reaction and a $\mathrm { F e } \left( \mathrm { C l O } _ { 4 } \right) _ { 3 }$ -mediated oxidative cascade cyclization reaction, respectively.

attracted attention due to their structural diversity and intriguing biological activities.<sup>1</sup> The total synthesis of several notable DMOA-derived meroterpenoids, including simplicissin (2), berkeleyone A (3), andrastin D (4), and related congeners, has been successfully achieved by the research groups of Porco, Jr., Maimone, Newhouse, Li, and Xie.<sup>2</sup>

In 2021, a novel DMOA-derived tri-nor-meroterpenoid janthinoid A (1)<sup>3</sup> was isolated from the tabacum-derived endophytic fungus Penicillium janthinellum TE-43 by Zhang and co-workers. Janthinoid A (1), characterized by a strained oxabicyclo[3.2.1]octane motif with four continuous quaternary stereogenic centers<sup>4</sup> and hallmark embedded rigid lactone, exhibits in vivo antitumor activities against NSCLC cells A549. The structure of 1 was elucidated through extensive NMR spectroscopic analysis and single-crystal X-ray single crystal difraction.

Given the challenges surrounding chemo-, regio-, and stereocontrolled assembly of the rigid oxabicyclo[3.2.1]octane core, we aimed to develop a novel and concise strategy for construction of this intriguing motif and to accomplish the total synthesis of 1.

In 2014, Abe and co-workers proposed a biosynthetic pathway of andiconin (6) from preandiloid C (5),<sup>5</sup> featuring a nonheme Fe-dependent oxygenase-initiated cascade radical cyclization via intermediates A and B (Figure 2a). In this work, we report our efort to accomplish these transformations and achieve a 14-step total synthesis of 1 in a protecting-group-free 12 manner.

Retrosynthetically, we envisaged that the oxabicyclo[3.2.1]- octane core of compound 1 could be constructed from ketoester C via a sequence of oxidation,<sup>6</sup> double bond isomerization,<sup>7</sup> 6-endo-trig radical cyclization, and intramolecular oxidative lactonization<sup>8</sup> (Figure 2b), with the siteselectivity based on the acidity of the C14 proton.<sup>9</sup> We expected to construct compound C with a Horner−Wadsworth−Emmons (HWE) reaction and a condensation from the trans-decalin aldehyde D, which could be stereoselectively constructed via a novel approach from propargyl ester E through a stepwise intramolecular 1,3-acyloxy migration<sup>10</sup> and an epoxide-initiated cationic π-cyclization reaction via intermediate F.<sup>11</sup> The propargyl ester F in turn could be constructed from geranylacetone.

In summary, the asymmetric, protecting-group-free synthesis of janthinoid A (1) is accomplished for the first time in 14 steps starting from the commercially available geranylacetone. The enabling tandem reactions of Lewis-acid-mediated cationic-allene-ene cyclization and $\mathrm { F e ( C l O _ { 4 } ) _ { 3 } }$ -mediated oxidative annulation provide novel ways for the synthesis of the trans-decalin-based aldehyde 11 and the oxabicyclo[3.2.1]- octane core 19 in a regio- and stereoselective manner. Application of the developed chemistry for the synthesis of other types of complex natural products is currently underway in our laboratories and will be reported in due course.
